🔒 PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

Martodosko Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

TL;DR: We do NOT record or store your actual voice/audio input on our servers. Audio processing happens 100% locally on your device. We only sync the settings (JSON parameters) if you choose to share them.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Email address, Username, and Profile Picture (if uploaded).
  • Usage Data: Device model, Android version, app version, crash logs, and feature usage statistics (to improve performance).
  • Content Data: Preset names, descriptions, tags, and parameter values (JSON config) that you explicitly choose to upload to the Community Library.
  • Payment Data: Processed securely by our third-party providers (Stripe/GCash/etc.). We do not see or store your credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the App services.
  • To authenticate users and manage subscriptions.
  • To moderate user-generated content (checking for spam/malicious presets).
  • To analyze trends and improve the audio engine’s stability.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted partners to operate the App:

  • Supabase: For database storage, authentication, and file hosting. Their privacy policy applies to data stored there.
  • GitHub: For source code repository management.
  • Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics (Optional): For monitoring app health and errors anonymously.

4. Data Security

  • All communications between the App and Server are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Sensitive credentials (API keys, JWT tokens) are stored in the Android Keystore System, isolated from other apps.
  • User passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt/scrypt) via Supabase Auth.

5. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal info, contact us immediately to delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location (GDPR/CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data ("Right to be Forgotten").

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@martodosko.studio.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated via in-app notification.
